Hi! My name is Myriam Collada

Also known as Kaala Baloo. I build games, and the systems that make them think.

I hold a BSc in Development of Video Games and Virtual Environments from UDIT, with a double major in Design and Programming, and I am now studying an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at Universidad Politécnica de Madrid. I have thirteen published games and a track record across more than ten hackathons and game jams, three of them first places.

My work centres on applied AI: intelligent agents, behaviour simulation and generative systems. I have been writing code since I was 8 years old, from Scratch games to 3D-printed robots running on Arduino, and I still take the same pleasure in getting a system to behave.
